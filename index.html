Flowers

Flowers are one of the most beautiful creations of nature. They come in various shapes, sizes, and colors, and each flower has its unique traits and significance.

Rose

The rose is a symbol of love and beauty. It is known for its vibrant colors and sweet fragrance.

  • Color: Red, pink, white, yellow
  • Fragrance: Sweet and strong
  • Petals: Soft and delicate

Sunflower

The sunflower is known for its bright yellow petals and its ability to turn towards the sun.

  • Color: Yellow
  • Height: Tall
  • Symbolism: Happiness and positivity

Tulip

The tulip is a popular spring flower that comes in a variety of colors and represents perfect love.

  • Color: Red, yellow, purple, white
  • Shape: Cup-shaped
  • Season: Spring
